## 👋 About Me

I'm an **AI Engineer & Data Scientist** at **Mettler Toledo**, building GenAI tools, LLM pipelines, autonomous agents, and enterprise web systems that eliminate manual overhead and unlock data intelligence at scale.

- 🎓 **Background**: Two years in Canada specializing in **Cloud Computing** and **Data Analysis** at Durham College, mentoring and tutoring engineering students.
- 🚀 **Industry Experience**: Previously engineered and shipped NLP/ML features into production on an **AWS Kubernetes (EKS)** cluster at a Toronto-based AI startup.
- 🎯 **Core Philosophy**: *First-principles rigor from end to end* — verifiable math, clean data architectures, hardened models, and resilient cloud-deployed applications.
